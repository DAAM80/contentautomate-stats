Privacy Policy: ContentAutomate Stats

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who uses it. ContentAutomate Stats is used only by the owner of the YouTube channels it connects to. It has no other users.

What it accesses. With the owner's consent, it requests read-only access to YouTube Data and YouTube Analytics (including revenue reports) for channels the owner manages. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything.

Where data goes. Access tokens and report results are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API data. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops all further reads.
